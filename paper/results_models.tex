\subsection{Result Interpretation}
Gradient Boosting with lag features is the best final model. It reaches test RMSE 22.78, MAE 16.92, and $R^2$ 0.663. Random Forest with lag is close, and Lasso with lag is the strongest linear model. This ranking gives a useful interpretation: nonlinear ensembles are best when maximum accuracy is required, but a simple regularized model becomes competitive once the dataset contains the right temporal features.

The most important result is not only the winning model. Lag features improve every model. The improvement is largest for linear models because lag variables add information that calendar and weather features could not express. Ensemble trees remain strongest because they can combine station, weather, calendar, and lag information through nonlinear splits.
