\section{Related Work and Comparable Projects}
Bike rental demand forecasting is a common regression benchmark because rental counts are influenced by calendar patterns, weather, and local mobility habits. The older UCI Bike Sharing Dataset uses Capital Bikeshare data from 2011--2012 and is widely used for hourly or daily rental prediction \cite{uci_bike}. Kaggle's Bike Sharing Demand competition uses a similar forecasting framing and shows that engineered time and weather features are standard for this task \cite{kaggle_competition}. Our project differs by using a newer Capital Bikeshare dataset from 2020/05--2024/08 and by modelling demand at station-day level instead of city-wide hourly demand \cite{taweilo_kaggle,capital_system_data}.
